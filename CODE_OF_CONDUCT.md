# Code of Conduct

Sonora follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/nerdyADITYA). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block participants who break this code.
